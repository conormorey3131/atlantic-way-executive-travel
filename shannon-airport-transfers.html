01Why Private

The stress-free way to and from Shannon

Shannon Airport is one of Ireland's easiest airports — and a private transfer keeps it that way. No taxi rank queues, no ride-share roulette, no squeezing luggage into whatever turns up. Just one trusted local driver in an immaculate Mercedes-Benz E-Class, booked in advance at a price you agreed before you set off.

Because Atlantic Way Executive Travel is based in Shannon itself, early collections are effortless — a 4am pickup in Ennis or Limerick for the first flight to Heathrow is all in a day's work. Landing late? Your flight is tracked, so a delay never means a missed pickup.

  • Door-to-door — collected at your home, hotel or office, dropped at the terminal
  • Flight tracking on every arrival, with waiting time built in for delays
  • Meet & greet in the arrivals hall with a name sign, on request
  • Child seats available with advance notice
  • Transfers to every Irish airport — Cork, Dublin, Knock and Kerry too

04Questions

Shannon Airport transfer FAQs

How much is a taxi from Shannon Airport to Ennis?

A private transfer from Shannon Airport to Ennis is a fixed price agreed when you book — no meter, no surge pricing. Send your date and time by phone or WhatsApp and you'll have a confirmed quote back in minutes.

Do you track flight delays?

Yes. Every airport pickup is tracked from departure, so if your flight into Shannon is early or delayed, Hugh adjusts the collection time automatically. You'll never be left waiting at arrivals and you'll never pay extra because your flight was late.

Can you collect us for a 4am flight?

Absolutely. Early-morning departures are a speciality — being based minutes from Shannon Airport means a 4am or 5am collection anywhere in Clare or Limerick is no problem. Advance bookings are guaranteed, whatever the hour.

Will you meet us inside the terminal?

Yes — meet and greet is available on request. Hugh will be waiting in the arrivals hall with a name sign, help with your bags, and walk you straight to the car. Ideal for first-time visitors, families and business travellers.

How many passengers and bags can you take?

The Mercedes-Benz E-Class comfortably seats up to 4 passengers with full-size luggage in the boot. For larger groups, get in touch — Hugh can often arrange a trusted second car.

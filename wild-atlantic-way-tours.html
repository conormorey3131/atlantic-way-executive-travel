01Why Private

Ireland's most spectacular coastline, without the coach

The Wild Atlantic Way runs right through County Clare — and some of its most breathtaking stretches are less than an hour from Shannon Airport. The Cliffs of Moher rising 214 metres sheer out of the ocean. The strange, beautiful limestone desert of the Burren. The wind-scoured lighthouse at Loop Head, where dolphins play in the Shannon Estuary below.

You could see it from a coach window, on someone else's timetable. Or you could travel the way visitors from the US, Canada and beyond tell us they wish they'd done from the start: a private car, a comfortable seat, and a local driver who'll pull in at the viewpoint the buses fly past, recommend the chowder in Doolin, and time the Cliffs for when the crowds thin out.

02The Tours

Popular day tours from Shannon & Clare

Every tour is private and fully flexible — these itineraries are simply the days guests love most. Mix, match or invent your own.

Cliffs of Moher & the Burren

The classic Clare day: the Cliffs of Moher, Doolin village, the coast road through the Burren, Poulnabrone Dolmen and Galway Bay views at Ballyvaughan. Approx. 6–8 hours.

Loop Head Peninsula

Clare's best-kept secret — the dramatic sea cliffs and lighthouse at Loop Head, the Bridges of Ross, Kilkee's cliff walk and dolphin country along the Shannon Estuary. Approx. 6–7 hours.

Galway City & the Coast

Along Galway Bay to the City of the Tribes — the Latin Quarter, Spanish Arch and Salthill prom, with time to shop, eat and wander before a comfortable run home. Approx. 6–8 hours.

Build Your Own

Bunratty Castle, Adare, the Ring of Kerry, Connemara, ancestral townlands — tell Hugh what's on your list and he'll shape a day (or several) around it.

04Questions

Private tour FAQs

How much does a private Cliffs of Moher tour cost?

Private day tours are priced per car, not per person — so for a couple or family the cost often compares well with bus tour tickets, with none of the crowds or fixed schedule. Send your dates by phone or WhatsApp for a fixed quote.

Can you collect us from our hotel or from Shannon Airport?

Yes — tours start from your hotel, B&B, holiday home or straight from Shannon Airport arrivals. Many visitors land in the morning and begin their Wild Atlantic Way tour the same day.

How long is a Wild Atlantic Way day tour?

Most tours run 6 to 8 hours, but it's your day — linger at the Cliffs of Moher, stop for lunch in Doolin, add a detour. Because it's a private tour, the itinerary flexes around you.

Are entrance fees included?

Attraction entrance fees (such as the Cliffs of Moher Experience) are paid separately, so you only pay for what you choose to visit. Hugh will advise on tickets worth booking ahead in high season.

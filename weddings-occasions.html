01Weddings

Wedding car hire across Clare & Limerick

From Dromoland Castle to the Falls Hotel, the Armada to the Inn at Dromoland, Clare's wedding venues are some of the most beautiful in Ireland — and Hugh knows the road to every one of them. Your wedding car arrives early, gleaming, with time built in for photographs, nerves and one last practice of the speech.

The package is simple: collection, ceremony, photographs and delivery to your reception, at a fixed price agreed when you book. Multiple runs for the bridal party can be arranged, and for guests flying in, Hugh can look after Shannon Airport transfers before and after the big day.

  • Bride, groom or bridal party — single or multiple runs to the ceremony
  • Photos with the car — as long as you need, wherever the light is best
  • Ribbons & finishing touches on request, in your colours
  • Guest logistics — airport collections and hotel shuttles for visiting family
